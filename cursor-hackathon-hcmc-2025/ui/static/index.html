Why this moment

Ads moved into the chat box. Agents move the infrastructure.

Thrad frames it plainly: ad infrastructure for AI — publishers monetize with in-chat placements; brands buy impressions in LLM-native channels. That stack monitors prompts, scores intent, ranks creative, and deploys sponsored answers in milliseconds. This hackathon is the agentic layer on top: who automates bids, who guards brand safety, who proves ROI back to the conversation — with humans clear on what ships autonomously.

In February 2026 OpenAI flipped the switch on ads inside ChatGPT — CPC bids of $3–$5, $200K minimum advertiser commitments, Expedia and Best Buy live on day one. Anthropic answered with Super Bowl spots saying “ads are coming to AI, but not to Claude.” Thrads co-hosts — same real-time bidding layer powering paid ads in LLMs, used by Fortune 500 brands and case-studied by AWS and BCG. You are building in Cursor — the strongest agentic IDE available — and the ask is concrete: ship agents that buy, sell, or measure attention inside conversations, and make it obvious what they will and will not do.

Track 01 · Advertiser side Buy-Side Agents

Buy-Side Agents

Agents that plan, bid, and buy ads inside LLM channels — ChatGPT-style placements, Thrad-powered apps, sponsored conversational answers. The risk is direct: misplaced spend, off-brand creative, or runaway budget. The human-in-the-loop question: when can the agent commit money alone, what counts as a brand-safety violation, and who signs off on creative before it serves?

  • Auto-bids on conversational intent, escalates spend spikes
  • Generates and rotates ad creative inside brand guardrails
  • Detects wasted spend, pauses unprofitable placements

Build for the advertiser: brands, agencies, DSPs.

Track 02 · Publisher side Sell-Side & Measurement

Sell-Side & Measurement

Agents that help AI publishers monetize and prove ROI — picking placements, scoring prompt-level intent, attributing conversions back to chat sessions. The risk is indirect: a bad signal becomes a bad billing event. The human-in-the-loop question: when is intent strong enough to monetize, and which conversions need a human-confirmed audit?

  • Scores prompt intent in real time, gates ad eligibility
  • Stitches attribution from chat → click → conversion
  • Flags suspicious traffic, asks before refunding spend

Build for the publisher / SSP / measurement side: AI apps, chatbots, attribution platforms.
